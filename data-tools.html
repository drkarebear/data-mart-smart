Which Data Source Should I Use?

DataVista, Data Mart, and local institutional systems overlap, but they are not interchangeable. The best source depends on the decision you are trying to support.

The three-source model

Think of these as complementary layers rather than competing databases.

Student story and multi-year context

DataVista

Start here for: student outcomes, equity, progression, completion, transfer, statewide initiatives, and broad program trends.

Strength: defined metrics, disaggregations, visualizations, snapshot and cohort views.

DataVista Made Simple

Detailed public MIS reporting

CCC Data Mart

Start here for: grade distribution, course details, traditional term-level reports, detailed public queries, and Data Mart exports.

Strength: precise report structures and direct table exports.

Data Mart Made Simple

Current campus operations

Local Institutional Data

Start here for: live enrollment, waitlists, fill rates, current section decisions, staffing, FTEF, room/time patterns, and locally defined populations.

Strength: recency and campus-specific context.

Same source data does not mean same measure

Both statewide systems rely heavily on data submitted through Chancellor's Office MIS, but each product can apply its own population rules, calculations, timeframes, and display logic. Match the metric definition before comparing numbers across systems.
