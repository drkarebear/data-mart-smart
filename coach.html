      What the Coach is deciding

      The routing logic follows the same sequence used throughout Data Mart Smart:

      Question → source → population → metric → denominator → interpretation → limitation → next question.

      What the three source choices mean

      Data Mart is often the simplest first stop for specific term, course, grade, section, student-count, service, award, or staffing reports. DataVista is strongest when the question needs cohorts, equity, pathways, completion, transfer, labor-market outcomes, or an integrated annual student-outcomes view. Local institutional data should lead when the decision depends on what is happening now, such as live enrollment, waitlists, fill rates, scheduling, staffing, or institution-specific definitions.

      Use recommendations as a starting point

      No statewide dashboard can tell you why a pattern occurred by itself. Use the Coach to choose a defensible first source and frame the next question; use local knowledge, institutional research, and current official definitions before making consequential decisions.
