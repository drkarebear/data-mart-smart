Use Data for Enrollment Management and Scheduling

Use historical and comparative data to understand participation and course-offering patterns, then combine that information with current local scheduling evidence.

What Data Can Help You Do

Data can help identify growth or decline, changes in course offerings, program scale, and instructional activity. It is especially useful for asking whether a pattern is persistent enough to influence scheduling strategy.

Useful Data Mart Starting Points

  • Annual/Term Student Count
  • Credit Courses/Sections
  • Course Details
  • FTES Summary
  • Distance Education FTES
  • multi-year section/enrollment trends

What to Pair With Data Mart

  • live class schedule and local SIS
  • fill rates and waitlists
  • cancellations and late-start sections
  • student demand information
  • faculty availability and room/modality constraints

Questions to Bring to the Conversation

  • Are fewer students participating, or are fewer sections being offered?
  • Do section counts and enrollment counts move together?
  • Are modality or scheduling changes part of the pattern?
  • Does local live enrollment tell the same story as historical Data Mart data?

Important Boundary

Data Mart is not a live scheduling system. Do not use lagged statewide reporting as a substitute for current enrollment, waitlist, cancellation, or room-capacity information.

Turn the Purpose Into a Query

If you know the decision or conversation but not the report, use Find the Right Report or CCC Data Mart Coach.
