Enrollment planning

Use Data for Enrollment Planning and Scheduling

For the next schedule, start local. For historical term-to-term evidence, Data Mart is often the simplest statewide follow-up.

1. Start with current local operations

  • current enrollment and capacity;
  • waitlists and unmet demand;
  • fill rates and cancellations;
  • time of day, modality, location, and late-start patterns;
  • course sequencing and bottlenecks;
  • current faculty availability and assignment constraints.

2. Use Data Mart for clean term comparisons

If you want Fall-to-Fall or term-specific history, Data Mart can be more direct than a broader annual dashboard.

3. Add DataVista when you need the broader annual student story

Use DataVista when you want annual program trends tied to student outcomes, cohort progression, equity, completion, or transfer rather than a simple term scheduling history.

Do not schedule from lagged statewide data alone

Neither DataVista nor Data Mart can tell you whether a section is filling this morning or whether a waitlist formed yesterday. Use current local data for the operational decision.
