Choose the success question before the platform

Use Data for Student Success and Pathways

“Student success” can mean a term-specific course rate, an annual program trend, or a longitudinal student journey. Those questions do not all belong in the same tool.

Which success question are you asking?

1

What was the success or retention rate in a specific term?

Use Data Mart. The Retention/Success report gives enrollment, retention, and success counts and rates for a selected term.

Open the official Retention/Success Rate report · Use the Course Success guide

2

How has one annual outcome changed over time, by program or subgroup?

Use DataVista. Single Metric is a strong choice when you know the exact outcome.

Open DataVista Single Metric

3

What happened to the same starting group of students?

Use a DataVista Cohort view. Cohorts are for progression of a defined starting group over time.

Choose Snapshot or Cohort

4

What does the broader pathway or statewide initiative show?

Use DataVista Reports or Metric Themes. Guided Pathways, Vision 2030, and related reports use defined statewide frameworks.

Open DataVista Reports

A course rate is not a student journey

A term success rate describes successful course enrollments in the selected report population. It does not automatically tell you whether the same students persisted, completed, or transferred.

Add local context before explaining why

Curriculum, course sequence, modality, scheduling, supports, faculty practices, and local interventions can matter. Statewide data can show a pattern; they do not establish the cause by themselves.
