Student success and pathways

Use Data for Student Success and Pathways

Start with DataVista when the question is about student progress, success, completion, transfer, or a pathway over time.

Choose the frame first

📸

Snapshot

Use for a selected-year picture of students meeting each metric's criteria.

🛤️

Cohort

Use when you want to follow the same defined starting group across time.

Good DataVista starting points

  • Metric Themes when you are exploring a broad segment of the student experience;
  • Single Metric when you know the exact outcome;
  • Guided Pathways for first-year leading indicators;
  • Comprehensive Student Report for a broad annual student-success picture.

Use Data Mart for targeted follow-up

Grade Distribution, detailed course information, and report-specific term tables can help answer narrower questions that emerge from the broader DataVista pattern.

Add local context before explaining why

Course sequence changes, faculty practices, scheduling, supports, modality, curricular redesign, student services, and local interventions can all matter. A statewide pattern is a starting point for inquiry, not a causal explanation.

Do not confuse a rate with a journey

A higher or lower success rate in one year does not automatically tell you whether the same students persisted, completed, or transferred. Use a cohort measure when the question is about progression of a defined group.
