Working snapshot: September 2026

Current Data and Legacy Reports

“Latest” can mean something different in every metric or report. Check the live selector and definition before calling a number current.

DataVista: check the metric, not just the dashboard

The 2026 Metric Definition Dictionaries include a Last Updated Date for individual metrics. That matters because different measures can refresh on different schedules.

Examples from the 2026 dictionary

Sections offered, enrollments, and online/DE sections are defined as Comprehensive Snapshot offerings metrics. The dictionary also includes a program-level calculated FTES metric with its own methodology and caveats.

Two DataVista freshness traps

  • Lagged or incomplete outcomes: some cohort measures need additional years before they are final.
  • Calculated FTES: the DataVista program-level FTES measure is not official CCFS-320 apportionment FTES.

Data Mart: “latest” still varies by report

Student, course, award, wage, transfer, and employee reports follow different submission and processing cycles. A missing newest term is not automatically an error.

Best practice

Open the live Data Mart report and verify the newest option in its time-period selector before describing a result as “current” or “latest.”

Legacy or historical Data Mart reports to recognize

Matriculation

A historical report family. Use newer student-success-services reporting for current service activity.

Transfer Velocity

A historical cohort tool. Use it for the cohort question it was designed to answer, not as a current transfer count.

Basic Skills Progress Tracker

Built around historical basic-skills pipelines. Read the methodology carefully before applying it to today's curriculum structures.

TOP is still important

Current statewide tools still use TOP-code program structures in many places. Do not treat a local department prefix and a TOP code as the same population.
