Why “Latest” Varies

Student, course, award, wage, transfer, and employee reports follow different submission and processing cycles. A missing newest term is not automatically an error.

Best Practice

Open the live report and verify the newest option in its time-period selector before describing a result as “current” or “latest.”

Legacy or Historical Reports to Recognize

Matriculation

The live selector currently ends at Spring 2014. For newer student-success-service activity, begin with Student Success Services.

Transfer Velocity

A historical cohort tool whose current cohort selector tops out at 2014–15. Use it for cohort questions, not as a current transfer count.

Basic Skills Progress Tracker

Still live, but built around historical basic-skills pipelines. Read the methodology carefully before applying it to today's curriculum structures.

TOP Is Still in the Current Data Mart

The system is transitioning from TOP to CIP, but current Data Mart reports remain TOP-based. Do not rewrite present-day instructions as though CIP has already replaced TOP.

Current Pages Override Old Training Materials

Older manuals can still be useful background, but when they conflict with the live 2026 interface or current Data Element Dictionary, use the current official source.
