Method and cautions














Best exports for District Compare


District Compare currently has report-specific support for Course Details, Program Awards, Success & Retention, Grade Distribution, and Student Headcount. Course Details is usually the best starting point for reported section comparisons.


Why not every Data Mart report yet?
Some Data Mart exports do not identify college, program, period, and measure in a structure that can be compared safely without additional testing. CCC Data Smart will not guess at those fields.
