Built for People With a Question

Data Mart Smart is designed for faculty, department chairs, classified professionals, staff, and administrators who need to use CCC data but may not work with institutional data every day.

Use Data to Ask Better Questions

A higher or lower number is not automatically evidence of a better or worse program, instructor, department, or college. Use Data Mart to notice patterns, add context, and decide what to investigate next.

Learn how to move from a number to a responsible conversation →

Why Use Data?

Data are most useful when they help us ask better questions, add context, and notice patterns worth investigating.

📈See patterns over time

Data Mart Smart is an independent, faculty-led educational project. It is not an official California Community Colleges Chancellor's Office or CCC Data Mart product and is not funded, sponsored, or endorsed by those entities. Guidance may contain errors or become outdated as Data Mart changes. Verify consequential findings against current official CCCCO documentation and appropriate local institutional sources.
