Understand the number →

The goal is not just a number. It is a number you can explain.

CCC Data Smart keeps distinctions such as students versus enrollments, snapshot versus cohort, reported sections versus locally understood classes, official versus calculated FTES, suppressed values, and incomplete years visible because those distinctions can change the meaning of a finding.

For consequential decisions, verify the result against the current metric definition and the appropriate local institutional source.

CCC Data Smart is an independent, faculty-led educational project.
