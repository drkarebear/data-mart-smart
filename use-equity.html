Before comparing groups

  1. Confirm the denominator. A subgroup outcome rate is not necessarily that subgroup's share of all students who met the outcome.
  2. Keep the student group consistent. Snapshot and Cohort answer different questions.
  3. Check suppression. Small values and complementary values may be masked.
  4. Check completeness. Recent cohort outcomes may still update.

A disparity is not a diagnosis

The data can establish that outcomes differ. They do not by themselves explain why. Add student experience, process, curriculum, scheduling, service-use, and other local evidence before attributing cause.

When Data Mart can help

If the follow-up question becomes “What grades were awarded?” use Data Mart Grade Distribution. If it becomes “What was the term-specific success rate?” use Data Mart Retention/Success Rate.
