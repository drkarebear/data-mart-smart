Explore a Data Mart Export

Open a report you downloaded from CCC Data Mart. Data Mart Smart identifies the report and turns supported exports into clearer filters, charts, accessible tables, cautions, and reusable method notes.

Your spreadsheet stays in this browser tab. Data Mart Smart reads the selected file locally with JavaScript. The spreadsheet itself is not uploaded to Data Mart Smart.

What This Tool Does, and Does Not Do


Explore Data makes an official Data Mart export easier to work with. It does not change the source data, create new official measures, or replace CCCCO documentation. The tool should help you see the measure, population, time period, grouping, and caution that belong with the number.




Keep the official source in the research trail


For consequential planning, reporting, accreditation, hiring, curriculum, or resource decisions, verify the result against the current Data Mart report, its Notes & Links information, and appropriate local institutional sources.
