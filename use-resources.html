Staffing & Resources

Data Mart can describe instructional activity, students, outcomes, and statewide staffing measures. It cannot decide what your college should hire or fund.

What are you preparing?

👩‍🏫 Faculty hiring request

Combine sections, course enrollments, FTES, trends, and local staffing evidence with your college's actual prioritization criteria.

Open faculty hiring guidance

🧰 Resource request

Describe the need, choose measures that actually speak to it, add local capacity or service evidence, and state what the resource would change.

Open resource request guidance
Watch for: A higher workload or enrollment value can support a descriptive claim. It does not by itself establish a staffing entitlement or resource allocation.
