# District Awards Trend update — September 6, 2026

District Compare & Trends now supports the intended one-spreadsheet workflow for Program Awards:

- upload one multi-year Program Awards Excel/CSV export;
- choose an award type, including A.A., A.A.-T, A.S., A.S.-T, or all award types when present;
- choose Latest 5 years, Latest 10 years, All available years, or a custom range;
- follow selected colleges on one multi-line chart;
- for Los Angeles CCD exports, all nine LACCD colleges are offered as selections, even if one college was not returned for the selected program/award type;
- missing college-period values remain missing and are never silently converted to zero.

The full official award-type text is retained in the method record even when the on-screen selector uses a shorter label.
