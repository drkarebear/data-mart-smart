What This Prototype Is Doing


The CCCCO export is hierarchical: district totals, college totals, award-type totals, then individual TOP-coded program rows. The viewer reconstructs that hierarchy and turns the program rows into a filterable dataset.


Current scope: Program Awards Summary Excel or CSV exports. The shared Explore Data tools also support several other tested Data Mart report structures.


Spreadsheet parsing uses SheetJS Community Edition 0.20.3, loaded from the official SheetJS CDN. Your selected workbook is processed in the browser and is not uploaded to Data Mart Smart.
