Institutional effectiveness and accreditation

Use Data for Institutional Effectiveness and Accreditation

Use statewide metrics for consistent public context, but keep local assessment, planning, qualitative, fiscal, and implementation evidence in the evidence set.

Choose the source that matches the claim

  • DataVista: statewide longitudinal student outcomes, equity, pathways, and established initiative metrics;
  • Data Mart: detailed public MIS reports and reproducible table exports;
  • Local institutional evidence: SLO/SAO assessment, strategic-plan measures, current operations, surveys, planning, budget, and implementation evidence.

Document the method

For every consequential figure, save the source, metric or report, student group, timeframe, locale, denominator, filters, retrieval date, and any suppression or completeness caveat.

Statewide data are not the institution's complete accreditation evidence set

They are one public source among local assessment, planning, qualitative, fiscal, governance, and implementation evidence. Use them to support a claim, not to stand in for the entire inquiry process.

Close the loop

Evidence is stronger when it connects to a decision, action, reassessment, and documented learning. A chart by itself is not institutional effectiveness.
