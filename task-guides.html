Task Guides

You do not need to know a Data Mart report name. Choose the real college task in front of you, and Data Mart Smart will show you which evidence can help, what local information to add, and what the numbers cannot decide for you.

The Pattern Behind Every Task Guide

Purpose → measure → Data Mart evidence → local evidence → limitation → next question. The goal is not simply to produce a number. It is to produce evidence you can explain, reproduce, and use responsibly.

Data Mart Smart

Data Mart Smart is an independent, faculty-led educational project. It is not an official California Community Colleges Chancellor's Office or CCC Data Mart product and is not funded, sponsored, or endorsed by those entities. Guidance may contain errors or become outdated as Data Mart changes. Verify consequential findings against current official CCCCO documentation and appropriate local institutional sources.
