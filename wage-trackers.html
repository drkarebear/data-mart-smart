Use the Wage Trackers Carefully

Wage reports can add useful labor-market context, but their measures, match rates, suppression rules, and time frames are not interchangeable.

Watch for

Suppression Matters

Wage cells can be omitted when there are too few matched wage records. The current wage trackers use a threshold of fewer than 10 matched wage records for suppression/omission.

What it means

A Wage Match Rate Is Not an Employment Rate

Unmatched Does Not Mean Unemployed

An unmatched award recipient may be missing from the wage source for many reasons. Treat match rate as a data-coverage measure, not as proof that unmatched graduates were unemployed.

Watch for

Before Comparing Wage Results

  • use the same tracker;
  • use the same TOP/program definition;
  • compare the same post-award time point;
  • consider match rates and suppression;
  • remember that wages are adjusted to constant dollars using CPI-U.
