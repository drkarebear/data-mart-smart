What the Visualization Shows


What it means


The chart shows how the reported grade records in your selected TOP area are distributed among the grade categories present in the export. The table underneath keeps the exact counts and percentages visible.


Grade Distribution is not Success & Retention
Use this report when you need grade composition. Use Enrollment Retention and Success Rate when you need CCCCO's grade-defined retention and success measures.






Data caution


Watch for
Keep Unusual Categories Visible


If an export contains a blank grade-category label, Data Mart Smart preserves it as Unlabeled / blank category. It does not silently discard the row or assume that blank means zero.




What Should You Ask After You See the Pattern?


What next


  • Would the Success & Retention report answer a summary-rate question better?
  • Are the populations comparable before you compare two colleges or terms?
  • Would course-level or modality context help explain the pattern?
