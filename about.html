Why this project exists

About Data Mart Smart

Data Mart Smart is an independent, faculty-led educational project designed to make California Community Colleges Data Mart easier to find, understand, and use responsibly.

Why This Exists

CCC Data Mart contains valuable public information, but finding the right report and understanding what a number actually represents can take more time than it should. A faculty member may know exactly what institutional question matters while having little reason to know the structure of MIS reporting or the difference among several similar-sounding measures.

Data Mart Smart starts from the user's question. The goal is to translate that question into a useful report, a defensible setup, and an interpretation that can be explained and reproduced.

What This Project Tries to Do

  • make common Data Mart tasks easier to navigate;
  • explain technical measures in plain language;
  • help users avoid double counting and mismatched comparisons;
  • show how to document a reproducible method;
  • separate what the data show from what they do not establish;
  • help users recognize when Data Mart is not the right source.

Independent Project

Not an Official CCCCO Product

Data Mart Smart and CCC Data Mart Coach are independent, faculty-led educational resources. They are not official California Community Colleges Chancellor's Office or CCC Data Mart products and are not funded, sponsored, or endorsed by those entities.

Use Official Sources for Verification

This project relies on current official CCCCO sources whenever possible, but reports, definitions, available terms, and interfaces can change. Guidance can also contain errors or omissions. For consequential institutional decisions, verify key findings against current official CCCCO documentation and appropriate local institutional research or data staff.

Data Mart Smart + CCC Data Mart Coach

The website teaches reusable methods and practical data-literacy habits. CCC Data Mart Coach is the conversational part of the project: users can bring a specific question, screenshot, or export and get help applying those methods.
