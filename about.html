Why this project exists

About CCC Data Smart

CCC Data Smart is an independent, faculty-led educational project designed to make California Community Colleges data easier to find, understand, compare, and use responsibly.

Why this exists

Faculty often begin with a clear institutional question but face several data systems, overlapping measures, unfamiliar populations, and technical definitions. The challenge is no longer only “Which Data Mart report?” It is also “Which source should I use at all?”

CCC Data Smart starts with the question and works toward the source, population, metric, denominator, interpretation, and limitation.

What this project tries to do

  • help users choose among DataVista, Data Mart, and local institutional data;
  • explain technical measures in plain language;
  • make snapshot, cohort, suppression, denominators, and incomplete years visible;
  • help users avoid double counting and mismatched comparisons;
  • show how to document a reproducible method;
  • separate what the data show from what they do not establish.

Independent project

Not an official CCCCO product

CCC Data Smart and CCC Data Coach are independent educational resources. They are not official California Community Colleges Chancellor's Office, DataVista, Data Mart, or local-college products and are not funded, sponsored, or endorsed by those entities.

Use official sources for verification

Reports, definitions, available years, and interfaces change. For consequential institutional decisions, verify key findings against current official CCCCO documentation and the appropriate local institutional research or data source.

Website + Coach

The website teaches reusable methods and practical data-literacy habits. The conversational Coach can help apply those methods to a specific question, screenshot, or export.
