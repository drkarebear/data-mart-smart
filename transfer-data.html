What it means

Transfer Velocity vs. Transfer Volume

How the transfer reports differ
ReportUse it for
Transfer VelocityLongitudinal transfer outcomes for a defined historical entering cohort over time.
Transfer VolumeCounts of transfers to in-state private and out-of-state baccalaureate-granting institutions; CSU volume is linked separately.

Transfer Velocity Is a Historical Cohort Tool

Do Not Treat It as a Current-Term Transfer Count

The live Transfer Velocity cohort selector currently tops out at 2014–15. That does not make the report broken; it means the report is designed around historical cohorts rather than current-term transfer volume.

Do this

Choose Based on the Question

  • “What proportion/count of a historical entering cohort transferred over time?” → Transfer Velocity
  • “How many students transferred to baccalaureate institutions?” → Transfer Volume

Watch for

Do Not Compare Them as Though They Share One Denominator

Transfer Velocity is cohort-based and longitudinal. Transfer Volume is a count-based transfer report. Their populations and time frames are different.
