Primary Location Can Matter

In Faculty & Staff Demographics, “primary location” is the college where the employee is assigned the greatest number of hours. A multi-college employee may therefore appear under one primary-location college rather than being counted independently at every place they work.

Know the Unit Before You Compare

Headcount, FTE, Assignment, and Salary Are Different Measures

Do not compare or combine staffing outputs until you know whether the report is counting employees, assignments, FTE, hours, salary measures, or another staffing unit.

A Good Staffing Workflow

  1. Define the staffing question

    For example: employee headcount, instructional FTE, demographic composition, salary, or assignment type.

  2. Choose the report whose unit matches the question

    Do not choose a report just because it contains the word “faculty.”

  3. Use the same fall year and geography

    Keep comparisons consistent across colleges or districts.

  4. Read the report-specific definitions

    Especially for primary location, FTE, and assignment-based measures.
