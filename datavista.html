DataVista Made Simple

DataVista is the Chancellor's Office statewide longitudinal metrics platform for student success. The trick is choosing the right view and population before you interpret the chart.

Best when: your question is tied to a statewide initiative or established reporting framework.

Why this matters

A snapshot question sounds like “What happened this year?” A cohort question sounds like “What happened to the students who started together?” Mixing those frames can produce a confident but incorrect interpretation.

For program and department questions

General Admit Snapshot metrics can include a sector/program drilldown, including TOP-code levels. That makes DataVista useful for broad program trends in offerings, enrollments, success, completion, transfer, and other student outcomes.

Calculated FTES is not official apportionment FTES

DataVista's calculated FTES metric uses a documented calculation for courses in the selected TOP code. The Metric Definition Dictionary explicitly says the methodology is not the CCFS-320 methodology and should not be used as official apportionment FTES.

Four things to check before you quote a DataVista number

  1. Student group. General Admit, cohort, dual enrollment, adult learners, or another defined group?
  2. Metric definition and denominator. Open the Metric Definition Dictionary, especially for rates.
  3. Complete or incomplete year. Some cohort outcomes can update as additional years of data become available.
  4. Suppression. Small groups may be masked, and complementary suppression can hide an additional group to protect privacy.

Why is a value missing?

Suppressed data are not the same as zero. DataVista generally masks small groups and can apply complementary suppression.

Common report starting points

Comprehensive Student Report

A strong program-review starting point for broad year-over-year student success and activity measures, including views for all students and K12 dual enrollment students.

Student Equity and Achievement Program

Use when the inquiry is specifically about statewide SEA metrics and disaggregated equity outcomes.

Guided Pathways

Use for first-year leading indicators for General Admit Cohort students.

Strong Workforce Program

Use for CTE/sector program outcomes and SWP-related metrics.

When DataVista is not enough

Use Data Mart when you need a specific detailed public MIS report such as grade distribution or course details. Use local institutional data when the question is about current scheduling, waitlists, staffing, FTEF, or other campus operations.
