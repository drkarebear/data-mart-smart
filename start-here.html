Before you start: five distinctions worth knowing
  • Distinct students are not the same as course enrollments.
  • Reported sections are not automatically the same as locally understood classes.
  • Awards granted are not automatically unique graduates.
  • Rates need their denominator.
  • Data Mart is reported state data, not a live local student information system.
