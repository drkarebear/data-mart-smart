What Can I Use CCC Data For?

⚠️ What Data Should Not Do by Themselves

Data should inform a decision, not impersonate the decision. A Data Mart result by itself should not be used to assign blame, evaluate an individual instructor, prove causation, determine program quality, justify a high-stakes personnel action, or replace local knowledge and student voice.
